GameHelper Radar source notice
================================

POE2Radar's map projection and map-frame behavior is derived from:
https://github.com/MordWraith/Gamehelper/tree/main/Plugins/Radar

Upstream snapshot used for this parity port:
554e8123d4997c4a5acbc8a4b19e1b585fe8b2d0

The port follows the projection behavior in Plugins/Radar/Helper.cs and
Plugins/Radar/Radar.cs: live-player-relative deltas, the 38.7-degree camera
angle, height-scaled map diagonal, calibrated large/minimap zoom baselines,
map center/shift/default-shift handling, and viewport culling.
